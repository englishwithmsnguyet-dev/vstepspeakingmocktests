B1 LEVEL
There are several disadvantages of keeping animals in zoos.
One major disadvantage is that animals often lack enough space. They cannot move around freely as they do in the wild. As a result, they may feel uncomfortable.
Another negative effect is that animals may suffer from stress. Living in an artificial environment is very different from their natural habitat. Therefore, they may develop health or behavioral problems.
One more drawback is that animals are separated from their natural environment. They cannot follow their normal lifestyle or natural instincts. This can affect their well-being.
One additional disadvantage is that animals may lose their natural survival skills. Since they receive food and care from humans every day, they do not need to hunt or protect themselves. As a result, they may find it difficult to survive in the wild.
In short, keeping animals in zoos can create several problems for their lives and well-being.

B2 LEVEL
There are several disadvantages of keeping animals in zoos.
One major disadvantage is the lack of space. Compared with their natural habitats, zoo enclosures are often much smaller. Consequently, animals cannot move freely or behave naturally.
Another negative effect is psychological stress. Living in captivity may prevent animals from following their natural instincts and social behaviors. As a result, they may experience boredom, frustration, or health problems.
One more drawback is that zoos separate animals from their natural environment. They lose opportunities to interact with ecosystems in the way they would in the wild. This can reduce their overall quality of life.
One additional disadvantage is that animals may gradually lose their natural survival skills. Since food, shelter, and protection are provided by humans, they no longer need to hunt, find resources, or avoid predators. Consequently, they may struggle to survive if they are returned to the wild.
In short, keeping animals in zoos can negatively affect both their physical and psychological well-being.
