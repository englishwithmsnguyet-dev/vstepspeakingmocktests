SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, if I had to suggest a book for my friend, I would recommend reading a science fiction book.

First of all, it’s an entertaining book because it contains many exciting stories and unusual ideas. This makes reading more interesting. So, she can enjoy the book more.

Secondly, it can improve her imagination because it introduces new worlds, advanced technology, and future life. This encourages her to think beyond real life. So, she can become more imaginative and creative.

I wouldn’t recommend a mystery novel because it may focus mainly on solving crimes. As for a romance novel, it's less suitable because it focuses mainly on relationships.

In short, I believe a science fiction book is the best choice for this situation.

Tóm lại, tôi tin rằng một cuốn sách khoa học viễn tưởng là lựa chọn tốt nhất trong tình huống này.

  • solve problems effectively
  • improve personal responsibility
  • save time
(Chủ đề: Có một vài lý do tại sao kỹ năng ra quyết định lại quan trọng.)
  • giải quyết vấn đề hiệu quả

 Note (Your own idea) → Enhance logical thinking and analysis. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Tăng cường tư duy logic và phân tích.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
There are some reasons why decision-making skills are important.

One major factor is that decision-making skills help people solve problems effectively. This is because they can choose the best solution in difficult situations. As a result, they can deal with problems more successfully.

Another contributing reason is that decision-making skills help people improve personal responsibility. In fact, they learn to think carefully before making choices. Therefore, they become more responsible for their actions.

One more underlying issue is that decision-making skills help people save time. This means that they can make choices more quickly and confidently. This helps them avoid wasting time on unnecessary decisions.

In short, there are some reasons why decision-making skills are important, as mentioned above.

Một vấn đề cơ bản nữa là các kỹ năng ra quyết định giúp mọi người tiết kiệm thời gian. Điều này có nghĩa là họ có thể đưa ra những lựa chọn nhanh chóng và tự tin hơn. Điều này giúp họ tránh lãng phí thời gian vào các quyết định không cần thiết.
