Topic 1: Let's talk about reading books (Hãy nói về việc đọc sách)
1. How often do you read books? (Bạn có thường xuyên đọc sách không?)
B1 LEVEL
I read books almost every day, especially in the evening before sleeping.
B2 LEVEL
I read books on a daily basis, usually dedicating at least thirty minutes in the evening to wind down before sleep.
2. What kind of books do you prefer reading? (Bạn thích đọc loại sách nào hơn?)
B1 LEVEL
I prefer reading comic books and short novels because they are entertaining and easy to understand.
B2 LEVEL
I prefer reading novels and self-help books because they offer deep insights into human nature and help me improve myself.
3. What do you learn from reading books? (Bạn học được điều gì từ việc đọc sách?)
B1 LEVEL
I learn many new English words and get useful information about different countries and cultures.
B2 LEVEL
Reading allows me to expand my vocabulary, improve my critical thinking, and gain a broader understanding of various cultures and histories.
Topic 2: Let's talk about bank service (Hãy nói về dịch vụ ngân hàng)
1. Do you have a bank account? Why or why not? (Bạn có tài khoản ngân hàng không? Tại sao có hoặc tại sao không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, I have a bank account. It is very convenient for receiving my salary and paying for shopping online.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, absolutely. I maintain a bank account because it is indispensable for receiving payments, managing my savings, and conducting digital transactions.
2. What kinds of banks are available in your hometown? (Những loại ngân hàng nào có sẵn ở quê hương của bạn?)
B1 LEVEL
In my hometown, there are many state banks like Vietcombank and some private banks like Techcombank.
B2 LEVEL
My hometown offers a variety of commercial banks, including major state-owned banks and private financial institutions like Vietcombank and Techcombank.
3. Do you prefer paying by cash or by credit card? Why? (Bạn thích thanh toán bằng tiền mặt hay thẻ tín dụng hơn? Tại sao?)
B1 LEVEL
I prefer paying by credit card or bank transfer because I do not like carrying a lot of cash in my wallet.
B2 LEVEL
I prefer cashless payments, such as credit cards or mobile banking, because it is safer, faster, and allows me to keep track of my expenses easily.
SITUATION (tình huống)
Your neighbor makes a lot of noise. There are three solutions: talking to your neighbor, moving to another place, and reporting to the police. Which one would you choose? (Người hàng xóm của bạn gây ra rất nhiều tiếng ồn. Có ba giải pháp: nói chuyện trực tiếp với hàng xóm, chuyển đi nơi khác, và báo cảnh sát. Bạn sẽ chọn giải pháp nào?)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, if my neighbor made a lot of noise, I would choose the solution of talking to my neighbor. First of all, talking directly is the most polite and friendly way. Sometimes, they do not know that they are making too much noise. Secondly, this option is free and quick. We can solve the problem in a few minutes without any trouble. I wouldn't choose moving to another place because it is very expensive, tiring, and takes a lot of time. As for reporting to the police, it is too extreme and can make our relationship with the neighbor very bad. In short, I think talking to the neighbor is the best first step.
B2 LEVEL
Well, to deal with a noisy neighbor, I believe the most appropriate solution is talking to the neighbor directly. First of all, direct communication is the most respectful and constructive approach. Often, neighbors are simply unaware that their activities are causing disturbance, and a polite chat can resolve the issue immediately. Second, this solution is the most efficient and cost-free way to resolve local disagreements. I would not recommend moving to another place because relocating is highly expensive, exhausting, and impractical for a minor neighborhood dispute. As for reporting to the police, it should only be a last resort. Taking legal or official action prematurely can ruin relations and create long-term tension between neighbors. In conclusion, addressing the issue face-to-face is the most reasonable and effective initial step.
TOPIC DEVELOPMENT (phát triển chủ đề)
Topic: There are several reasons for divorce.
  • lack of responsibility
  • financial burden
  • communication problems
(Chủ đề: Có một vài lý do dẫn đến ly hôn.)
  • thiếu trách nhiệm
  • gánh nặng tài chính
  • vấn đề giao tiếp
 Note (Your own idea) → Infidelity and loss of trust. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Sự phản bội và mất niềm tin.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
There are several reasons why couples get divorced nowadays. First of all, a lack of responsibility causes problems. If one partner does not help with house chores or children, the other will feel tired and sad. Secondly, the financial burden is a big reason. When couples do not have enough money for daily life, they argue a lot. Thirdly, communication problems lead to divorce. If people do not talk to each other, they cannot understand each other's feelings. Finally, infidelity and loss of trust also cause divorce. When one partner is not honest, it is very hard to stay together. In short, lack of responsibility, money issues, poor communication, and loss of trust are the main reasons for divorce.
B2 LEVEL
There are various significant factors that lead to divorce in modern society. First, a lack of responsibility often strains a marriage. When one partner fails to contribute to household duties or parenting, it creates inequality and resentment. Second, a financial burden is a primary cause of marital breakdown. Financial stress leads to constant arguments and places immense pressure on the relationship. Third, communication problems are critical. Without open and honest conversations, couples drift apart and fail to resolve their issues. Lastly, infidelity and loss of trust play a major role in divorces. Once trust is broken due to dishonesty or unfaithfulness, rebuilding the relationship becomes extremely difficult. In conclusion, lack of responsibility, financial pressure, communication barriers, and broken trust are the leading causes of divorce.
FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (câu hỏi mở rộng)
1. What are some advantages of getting married? (Một số lợi ích của việc kết hôn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
Getting married helps us have a partner to share life with, raise happy children, and feel safe and loved.
B2 LEVEL
Marriage provides emotional support, a companion to share life's responsibilities, and a stable environment to raise children.
2. How can people maintain a happy marriage? (Làm thế nào để mọi người duy trì cuộc hôn nhân hạnh phúc?)
B1 LEVEL
People can keep a happy marriage by talking honestly, helping each other with daily work, and spending time together.
B2 LEVEL
Maintaining a happy marriage requires open communication, mutual respect, compromise, and quality time spent together.
3. Some people say young people today don’t want to get married. What do you think about this? (Một số người nói giới trẻ ngày nay không muốn kết hôn. Bạn nghĩ sao về điều này?)
B1 LEVEL
I think that is true because many young people want to focus on their careers first and enjoy their freedom.
B2 LEVEL
It is true that many young adults delay or avoid marriage. This is often because they prioritize career development, financial stability, and personal independence over traditional family roles.
