SPEAKING MOCK TEST 17
Topic 1: Let's talk about drinks (Hãy nói về đồ uống)
1. Do you have a favorite drink? (Bạn có loại đồ uống yêu thích nào không?)
B1 LEVEL
My favorite drink is orange juice because it is sweet, tasty, and very healthy.
B2 LEVEL
My favorite beverage is definitely freshly squeezed orange juice because it is refreshing, delicious, and loaded with vitamin C.
2. Do you often try different kinds of drinks? Why or why not? (Bạn có thường thử các loại đồ uống khác nhau không? Tại sao có hoặc tại sao không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, I do. I like to try new fruit juices and bubble teas when I go out with my friends.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, absolutely. I enjoy experimenting with new herbal teas, specialty coffees, and seasonal fruit smoothies to discover unique flavors.
3. What drink did you have most recently? (Đồ uống gần đây nhất bạn dùng là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
I drank a cup of hot green tea this morning after my breakfast.
B2 LEVEL
The most recent beverage I had was a cup of warm green tea right after having my breakfast earlier today.
Topic 2: Let's talk about crowded places (Hãy nói về những nơi đông người)
1. Have you ever been to a crowded place? (Bạn đã từng đến một nơi đông người chưa?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, I have. I went to a crowded shopping mall in Hanoi during the Tet holiday.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, I have been to several, notably a very crowded shopping mall in Hanoi during the peak holiday season.
2. How did you feel when you were there? (Bạn cảm thấy thế nào khi ở đó?)
B1 LEVEL
I felt a bit tired and overwhelmed because of the loud noise and too many people around.
B2 LEVEL
I felt rather exhausted and claustrophobic due to the high volume of noise and the endless crowds.
3. Do you prefer crowded places or quiet places? Why? (Bạn thích những nơi đông người hay những nơi yên tĩnh hơn? Tại sao?)
B1 LEVEL
I prefer quiet places because I want to relax, read books, and study without any noise.
B2 LEVEL
I strongly prefer quiet places because they provide a peaceful environment where I can unwind, read, and focus on my work.
SITUATION (tình huống)
Your neighbor has an 11-year-old son who is on summer vacation. She asks for your suggestion on where she can take him out for the day. There are three options: a beach, a science center, and a park. Which one would you suggest? (Người hàng xóm của bạn có một đứa con trai 11 tuổi đang trong kỳ nghỉ hè. Cô ấy hỏi ý kiến của bạn về nơi có thể dẫn cậu bé đi chơi trong ngày. Có ba lựa chọn: bãi biển, trung tâm khoa học, và công viên. Bạn sẽ đề xuất nơi nào?)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, if my neighbor wanted to take her 11-year-old son out, I would suggest the science center. First of all, an 11-year-old boy is very curious. The science center has many interactive and educational activities that can keep him interested. Secondly, it is an indoor place, so they do not have to worry about hot weather or sudden rain. I wouldn't choose the beach because it can be dangerous for a young boy to swim without many supervisors, and it takes too much travel time. As for the park, it is nice, but it can be boring after an hour since there are not many new things to learn there. In short, I think the science center is the best choice for a fun and educational day.
B2 LEVEL
Well, to suggest a great day trip for my neighbor's 11-year-old son, I would recommend the science center. First, at the age of 11, children are naturally curious and eager to learn. A science center offers a range of interactive experiments and hands-on exhibits that make learning extremely fun and engaging. Second, it is a safe and weather-proof indoor environment, ensuring the trip won't be disrupted by summer heat or unexpected rain. I would not recommend the beach because of the potential safety hazards regarding swimming unsupervised, alongside the long travel distance. As for the park, while it offers fresh air, it may lack the intellectual stimulation that a growing 11-year-old boy needs for a whole day out. In conclusion, the science center is the most rewarding and practical option.
TOPIC DEVELOPMENT (phát triển chủ đề)
Topic: Many people believe that setting goals plays an important role in life.
  • measurable progress
  • self-motivation
  • planning for the future
(Chủ đề: Nhiều người tin rằng việc đặt mục tiêu đóng vai trò quan trọng trong cuộc sống.)
  • sự tiến bộ có thể đo lường được
  • động lực tự thân
  • lên kế hoạch cho tương lai
 Note (Your own idea) → Improve time management. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Cải thiện quản lý thời gian.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Setting goals is very important in life for several reasons. First of all, goals help us track measurable progress. We can see how much we have achieved over time. Secondly, goals bring self-motivation. They give us energy and focus to work hard every day. Thirdly, setting goals is essential for planning for the future. We know what we want to do and where we want to go. Finally, goals help improve our time management. We can prioritize important tasks and avoid wasting time. In short, setting goals helps us track progress, stay motivated, plan our future, and manage time better.
B2 LEVEL
Goal-setting is widely regarded as a fundamental practice for personal and professional growth. First, goals provide measurable progress. They allow individuals to monitor their advancement and adjust their efforts accordingly. Second, having clear targets boosts self-motivation. It gives people a sense of purpose and the determination to overcome obstacles. Third, goals are crucial for planning for the future. They guide our long-term decisions and career choices. Lastly, setting goals significantly improves time management. By identifying key priorities, individuals can allocate their time efficiently and avoid distractions. In conclusion, tracking progress, boosting motivation, planning ahead, and optimizing time usage are the main reasons why setting goals is so vital.
FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (câu hỏi mở rộng)
1. Do you think short-term or long-term goals are more useful? Why? (Bạn nghĩ mục tiêu ngắn hạn hay dài hạn hữu ích hơn? Tại sao?)
B1 LEVEL
I think both are useful. Short-term goals help us take action immediately, while long-term goals give us a direction for the future.
B2 LEVEL
I believe both types of goals are equally essential. Short-term goals keep us focused on immediate actions, whereas long-term goals define our ultimate destination and give meaning to daily tasks.
2. What can happen if someone lives without any goals? (Điều gì có thể xảy ra nếu ai đó sống không có bất kỳ mục tiêu nào?)
B1 LEVEL
Without goals, a person might feel lost, bored, and waste a lot of time doing things that do not help them improve.
B2 LEVEL
Living without goals can lead to a lack of direction, causing individuals to feel demotivated, unproductive, and drift through life without achieving their true potential.
3. Should schools teach students how to set and achieve goals? (Trường học có nên dạy học sinh cách đặt và đạt được mục tiêu không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, they should. Teaching students how to set goals helps them study better and prepare for their future careers.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, absolutely. Educating students on goal-setting practices equips them with self-discipline and planning skills, which are highly beneficial for their academic and career success.
