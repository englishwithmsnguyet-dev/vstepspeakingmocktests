SPEAKING MOCK TEST 18
Topic 1: Let's talk about social events (Hãy nói về các sự kiện xã hội)
1. How often do you go to social events? (Bạn có thường xuyên đi đến các sự kiện xã hội không?)
B1 LEVEL
I go to social events about once a month, usually when my friends invite me to birthday parties.
B2 LEVEL
I attend social events about once a month, especially birthday celebrations or university gatherings.
2. What is your favorite social event? (Sự kiện xã hội yêu thích của bạn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
My favorite social event is a birthday party because I can meet my old friends and eat delicious cake.
B2 LEVEL
My favorite social event is a birthday party or reunion because it is a wonderful opportunity to reconnect with old friends and share stories.
3. Do you prefer small gatherings or large gatherings? Why? (Bạn thích những cuộc tụ họp nhỏ hay cuộc tụ họp lớn hơn? Tại sao?)
B1 LEVEL
I prefer small gatherings because they are quieter and I can talk to everyone easily.
B2 LEVEL
I prefer small gatherings because they offer a more intimate and quiet setting, allowing for deeper and more meaningful conversations.
Topic 2: Let's talk about cooking (Hãy nói về việc nấu ăn)
1. How often do you cook at home? (Bạn có thường xuyên nấu ăn ở nhà không?)
B1 LEVEL
I cook at home almost every day. I like preparing meals for my family.
B2 LEVEL
I cook at home on a daily basis, as I find preparing my own meals healthier and more relaxing.
2. What is your favorite dish to cook? (Món ăn yêu thích bạn thường nấu là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
My favorite dish to cook is fried rice because it is easy, fast, and I can add vegetables and eggs.
B2 LEVEL
My favorite recipe is fried rice with mixed vegetables, which is quick to prepare and customizable.
3. When did you first learn to cook? (Bạn học nấu ăn lần đầu khi nào?)
B1 LEVEL
I first learned to cook when I was 15 years old. My mother taught me how to make simple dishes.
B2 LEVEL
I first learned to cook when I was around 15 years old, under the guidance of my mother, who taught me how to prepare basic Vietnamese family meals.
SITUATION (tình huống)
You are going to have a community holiday and plan to take a short trip with your family. There are three options for planning the trip: plan the trip by yourself, ask a travel agency to plan the trip for you, and ask a friend who had a similar trip to plan it for you. Which one do you think is the best option? Why? (Bạn sắp có một kỳ nghỉ cộng đồng và dự định thực hiện một chuyến đi ngắn với gia đình. Có ba lựa chọn để lên kế hoạch cho chuyến đi: tự lên kế hoạch, nhờ công ty du lịch lên kế hoạch cho bạn, và nhờ một người bạn từng đi chuyến đi tương tự lên kế hoạch cho bạn. Bạn nghĩ lựa chọn nào là tốt nhất? Tại sao?)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, for our family holiday trip, I think the best option is to plan the trip by myself. First of all, planning by myself gives us **full flexibility**. We can choose where to go, when to rest, and what to eat based on our family's preferences. Secondly, it is the **most cost-effective** option because we do not have to pay extra fees to an agency. I wouldn't choose to ask a travel agency because their schedules are usually **too tight and rushed**, which is not relaxing for a family trip. As for asking a friend, it is helpful, but their preferences and budget might be **very different** from my family's. In short, planning the trip myself is the most personalized and enjoyable choice.
B2 LEVEL
Well, to organize a satisfying family holiday trip, I believe the best approach is to plan the trip by myself. First, it offers **absolute freedom and flexibility**. We can customize the itinerary, select specific accommodations, and change plans spontaneously according to my family's pace. Second, it is **highly cost-effective**, eliminating service fees and commission costs associated with agencies. I would not recommend asking a travel agency because they often offer **rigid, pre-packaged itineraries** that don't allow for personal relaxation. As for asking a friend, while their advice is valuable, their travel style and budget might not align with my family's current requirements. In conclusion, **self-planning** is the most rewarding way to travel with family.
TOPIC DEVELOPMENT (phát triển chủ đề)
Topic: Playing team sports brings many benefits.
  • keep fit
  • make friends
  • learn new skills
(Chủ đề: Chơi các môn thể thao đồng đội mang lại nhiều lợi ích.)
  • giữ dáng
  • kết bạn
  • học hỏi kỹ năng mới
 Note (Your own idea) → Improve mental health. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Cải thiện sức khỏe tinh thần.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Playing team sports brings many great benefits to our lives. First of all, sports help us keep fit. Running and playing regularly make our bodies stronger and healthier. Secondly, we can make new friends. Playing in a team helps us meet people who share similar interests. Thirdly, team sports teach us to learn new skills, such as communication, cooperation, and working with others. Finally, it helps improve our mental health. Playing sports is a fun way to reduce stress after long hours of study or work. In short, team sports are excellent for fitness, making friends, learning cooperation, and reducing stress.
B2 LEVEL
Engaging in team sports offers significant physical and psychological advantages. First, it is an excellent way to keep fit. Regular physical activity boosts cardiovascular health, strength, and stamina. Second, team sports provide a platform to make friends and socialize, building a strong sense of community and belonging. Third, players learn essential life skills such as teamwork, leadership, and effective communication under pressure. Lastly, participating in sports greatly improves mental health. It releases endorphins, which reduce stress, anxiety, and enhance overall mood. In conclusion, physical fitness, social connections, skill development, and mental well-being are the primary benefits of team sports.
FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (câu hỏi mở rộng)
1. What are some popular team sports in your country? (Một số môn thể thao đồng đội phổ biến ở nước bạn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
In Vietnam, football is the most popular team sport. Many people also play volleyball and badminton in the afternoons.
B2 LEVEL
In my country, football is undoubtedly the most popular sport. Additionally, volleyball, basketball, and badminton enjoy high participation rates among the youth.
2. Do you think children should be encouraged to play team sports? Why or why not? (Bạn có nghĩ trẻ em nên được khuyến khích chơi các môn thể thao đồng đội không? Tại sao có hoặc tại sao không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, they should. Playing team sports helps children stay healthy and learn how to share and work with their classmates.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, absolutely. Encouraging children to play team sports is highly beneficial as it teaches them cooperation, discipline, and healthy competition from an early age.
3. How can people be encouraged to do exercise regularly? (Làm thế nào để khuyến khích mọi người tập thể dục thường xuyên?)
B1 LEVEL
We can encourage people by building more free parks with exercise machines and organizing running events in cities.
B2 LEVEL
People can be motivated through the installation of public outdoor gym facilities, wellness campaigns at workplaces, and community fitness challenges.
