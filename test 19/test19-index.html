Topic 1: Let's talk about friends (Hãy nói về bạn bè)
1. Do you think you are a good friend? (Bạn có nghĩ mình là một người bạn tốt không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, I think I am a good friend because I always listen to my friends and help them when they have problems.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, I consider myself a good friend because I am supportive, honest, and always ready to lend a helping hand when my friends are in need.
2. What kind of people do you like to make friends with? (Bạn thích kết bạn với những người như thế nào?)
B1 LEVEL
I like to make friends with people who are kind, honest, and have a good sense of humor.
B2 LEVEL
I prefer making friends with individuals who are trustworthy, positive, and share similar interests.
3. Do you still keep in touch with your primary school friends? (Bạn có còn giữ liên lạc với bạn bè tiểu học không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, I do. We still meet occasionally and chat on Facebook or Zalo.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, I do. Although we are busy, we still keep in touch via social media and meet up once in a while.
Topic 2: Let's talk about public events (Hãy nói về các sự kiện công cộng)
1. What is your favorite public event? (Sự kiện công cộng yêu thích của bạn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
My favorite public event is the annual flower festival in my city because of the beautiful decorations.
B2 LEVEL
My favorite public event is the annual flower exhibition in town because it attracts many visitors and features beautiful displays.
2. Do you think public events should be organized regularly? Why or why not? (Bạn có nghĩ các sự kiện công cộng nên được tổ chức thường xuyên không? Tại sao có hoặc tại sao không?)
B1 LEVEL
Yes, they should. They give people a place to relax, have fun, and enjoy holidays with family.
B2 LEVEL
Yes, absolutely. Regular public events foster community spirit and offer a healthy source of entertainment for local residents.
3. What are some popular public events in your country? (Một số sự kiện công cộng phổ biến ở nước bạn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
In Vietnam, popular events are Tet flower markets, book festivals, and countdown parties on New Year's Eve.
B2 LEVEL
In Vietnam, popular public events include traditional spring festivals, book fairs, and cultural street parades.
SITUATION (tình huống)
You are planning to buy a new house. There are three aspects to consider: the size of the house, the cost of the house, and the location of the house. Which one do you think is the most important factor when choosing a house? Why? (Bạn đang lên kế hoạch mua một ngôi nhà mới. Có ba khía cạnh cần xem xét: kích thước ngôi nhà, chi phí ngôi nhà, và vị trí ngôi nhà. Bạn nghĩ yếu tố nào là quan trọng nhất khi chọn nhà? Tại sao?)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, when buying a new house, I think the most important factor is its location. First of all, a good location makes our daily life much easier. If the house is **close to my office and schools**, we can save a lot of travel time. Secondly, the location determines **safety and security**. Living in a good neighborhood is very important for my family's peace of mind. I wouldn't prioritize the size of the house because a house can be small but still cozy. We do not need too much space if we cannot afford it. As for the cost, it is important, but a cheap house in a bad or far location is **not worth it** in the long term. In short, I think location is the primary factor when choosing a home.
B2 LEVEL
Well, when purchasing a house, I consider the location to be the most critical factor. First, location dictates **convenience and accessibility**. Living near workspaces, good schools, hospitals, and supermarkets saves significant commuting time and reduces stress. Second, location is the main driver of **long-term property value**. Houses in prime locations tend to appreciate much faster over time. I would not prioritize the size of the house because space can be optimized with smart furniture, whereas location cannot be changed. As for the cost, although budget constraints are real, choosing a cheaper house in a remote or unsafe area will likely incur higher transport costs and security concerns later. In conclusion, location is the most important element for convenience, safety, and investment potential.
TOPIC DEVELOPMENT (phát triển chủ đề)
Topic: There are some disadvantages of self-employment.
  • financial burden
  • lack of benefits
  • increased responsibility
(Chủ đề: Có một số nhược điểm của việc tự làm chủ.)
  • gánh nặng tài chính
  • thiếu phúc lợi
  • tăng thêm trách nhiệm
 Note (Your own idea) → Unstable income. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Thu nhập không ổn định.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Although self-employment has benefits, there are several disadvantages to consider. First of all, self-employed people face a heavy financial burden. They have to spend their own money to start and run the business. Secondly, there is a lack of benefits. They do not get paid holidays, sick leave, or health insurance from a company. Thirdly, they have increased responsibility. They must do everything themselves, from marketing to accounting. Finally, they have to deal with an unstable income. If there are no customers, they do not earn any money. In short, financial burdens, lack of benefits, high responsibilities, and unstable income are the main drawbacks.
B2 LEVEL
While self-employment offers freedom, it also brings significant challenges. First, it carries a high financial burden. Self-employed individuals must secure their own funding and take full financial risks. Second, there is a complete lack of corporate benefits. They lack health insurance, pension contributions, and paid leaves. Third, they face increased responsibility. Managing all aspects of the business, from operations to client relations, can lead to high stress. Lastly, they must cope with an unstable and irregular income. Earnings fluctuate based on market demand and client availability, making financial planning difficult. In conclusion, financial risks, lack of social benefits, heavy workload, and irregular cash flow are the primary disadvantages of self-employment.
FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (câu hỏi mở rộng)
1. In your opinion, should students start their own business right after graduation? (Theo ý kiến của bạn, học sinh có nên tự kinh doanh ngay sau khi tốt nghiệp không?)
B1 LEVEL
I think they should work for a company first to gain experience and save some money before starting a business.
B2 LEVEL
No, I wouldn't recommend it. It is generally better for graduates to gain industry experience and build a professional network before taking on entrepreneurial risks.
2. Do you think it’s better to be self-employed or to work for a company? (Bạn nghĩ tự làm chủ hay làm việc cho một công ty tốt hơn?)
B1 LEVEL
It depends on the person. If you like safety, working for a company is better. If you like freedom, self-employment is better.
B2 LEVEL
It depends on individual preferences. Employment offers financial stability and structure, while self-employment provides independence and unlimited earning potential.
3. What qualities or skills are necessary for someone to be successfully self-employed? (Những phẩm chất hoặc kỹ năng nào là cần thiết để một người tự làm chủ thành công?)
B1 LEVEL
They need to be hardworking, patient, and have good financial planning skills.
B2 LEVEL
Key success factors include self-discipline, resilience, financial literacy, and strong problem-solving abilities.
