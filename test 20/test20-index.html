SPEAKING MOCK TEST 20
Topic 1: Let's talk about reading books (Hãy nói về việc đọc sách)
1. What is your favourite kind of book? (Loại sách yêu thích của bạn là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
My favorite kind of book is science fiction because it is full of imagination.
B2 LEVEL
I prefer reading science fiction novels because they spark my curiosity and creativity.
2. Why do some young people prefer reading online books? (Tại sao một số bạn trẻ thích đọc sách trực tuyến hơn?)
B1 LEVEL
Many young people like online books because they are free, portable, and easy to read on smartphones.
B2 LEVEL
Young adults often favor digital books due to their accessibility, cost-efficiency, and convenience on mobile devices.
3. How can we encourage children to read books? (Làm thế nào chúng ta có thể khuyến khích trẻ em đọc sách?)
B1 LEVEL
We can encourage them by buying colorful books and reading bedtime stories with them.
B2 LEVEL
Parents and teachers can foster a reading habit by offering highly illustrated books and setting a positive reading example.
Topic 2: Let's talk about charity work (Hãy nói về công việc từ thiện)
1. How often do you donate money or do charity work? (Bạn có thường xuyên quyên góp tiền hoặc làm từ thiện không?)
B1 LEVEL
I donate money or old clothes about twice a year to help poor children.
B2 LEVEL
I participate in charity drives a few times a year, donating funds or clothing to support underprivileged students.
2. Who can take part in charity activities? (Ai có thể tham gia vào các hoạt động từ thiện?)
B1 LEVEL
I think everyone can take part in charity, regardless of their age or income.
B2 LEVEL
In my view, anyone can engage in volunteer and charity work, as even small efforts count.
3. What are the benefits of doing charity work? (Lợi ích của việc làm từ thiện là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
It helps poor people have a better life and makes us feel happy and useful.
B2 LEVEL
Charity work not only uplifts marginalized communities but also instills empathy and a sense of fulfillment in volunteers.
SITUATION (tình huống)
Your cousin is going to university. There are three options to consider when choosing which university to attend: affordable fees, on-campus facilities, and student support services. Which one do you think is the most important for your cousin? Why? (Em họ của bạn chuẩn bị vào đại học. Có ba lựa chọn cần cân nhắc khi chọn trường đại học: học phí phải chăng, cơ sở vật chất trong khuôn viên trường, và dịch vụ hỗ trợ sinh viên. Bạn nghĩ điều nào là quan trọng nhất đối với em họ của mình? Tại sao?)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
Well, for my cousin going to university, I think the most important factor is affordable fees. First of all, affordable tuition fees help **reduce financial pressure** on her parents. University can be very expensive. Secondly, it allows her to focus on studying without worrying about finding a part-time job to pay for bills. I wouldn't prioritize on-campus facilities because while libraries and gyms are great, we can still study well with standard classrooms. As for student support services, they are useful, but they are not the primary concern for a student's daily academic life. In short, I think affordable fees are the most critical factor for my cousin.
B2 LEVEL
Well, for my cousin choosing a university, I believe the most important consideration is affordable fees. First, reasonable tuition fees significantly **minimize the financial burden** on the family, preventing huge student debts after graduation. Second, financial peace of mind allows students to **concentrate fully on their academic goals** rather than working long part-time hours. I would not prioritize on-campus facilities because basic learning resources are generally sufficient, and extra amenities are secondary. As for student support services, they are highly beneficial but cannot outweigh the critical importance of a manageable financial plan. In conclusion, **affordable tuition fees** are the most crucial factor for a stable university experience.
TOPIC DEVELOPMENT (phát triển chủ đề)
Topic: Nursery education should be mandatory.
  • build children’s confidence and cooperation
  • share educational responsibilities with parents
  • prepare children for primary school
(Chủ đề: Giáo dục mầm non nên là bắt buộc.)
  • xây dựng sự tự tin và hợp tác của trẻ
  • chia sẻ trách nhiệm giáo dục với cha mẹ
  • chuẩn bị cho trẻ vào trường tiểu học
 Note (Your own idea) → Improve social communication skills. (Ý kiến của riêng bạn → Cải thiện kỹ năng giao tiếp xã hội.)
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
I strongly believe that nursery education should be mandatory for several reasons. First of all, it helps build children's confidence and cooperation. Kids learn how to play, share, and make friends with others. Secondly, it helps share educational responsibilities with parents, especially working mothers and fathers who are very busy. Thirdly, nursery education prepares children for primary school by teaching them basic letters and numbers. Finally, it helps improve their social communication skills. Children learn how to speak clearly and listen to their teachers. In short, early education is essential for confidence, sharing duties, primary school prep, and communication.
B2 LEVEL
I strongly advocate for mandatory nursery education due to its extensive developmental benefits. First, it plays a vital role in building children's confidence and cooperation skills, as they interact with peers in a structured environment. Second, it helps share educational responsibilities with parents, supporting modern families where both parents work. Third, nursery schooling effectively prepares children for primary school, easing the transition to formal education. Lastly, it significantly improves social communication skills, teaching toddlers how to express themselves clearly and interact respectfully. In conclusion, mandating nursery education is crucial for child development, parental support, academic preparation, and social skills.
FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (câu hỏi mở rộng)
1. At what age should children start early education? (Trẻ em nên bắt đầu giáo dục sớm ở độ tuổi nào?)
B1 LEVEL
I think children should start early education at around 3 years old, when they can speak well.
B2 LEVEL
In my opinion, children should begin nursery school at about 3 years of age, as they are developmentally ready to socialize.
2. What are the advantages and disadvantages of early education? (Những ưu điểm và nhược điểm của giáo dục sớm là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
The advantage is that children learn social skills early, but the disadvantage is that they might feel stressed and miss family time.
B2 LEVEL
The key advantage is accelerated cognitive and social growth, while the drawback is potential separation anxiety and pressure at too young an age.
3. What are some differences between education now and in the past? (Một số khác biệt giữa giáo dục hiện nay và trước đây là gì?)
B1 LEVEL
Now, students use computers and tablets, and the lessons are more practical, while in the past, students only used paper books.
B2 LEVEL
Today's education integrates modern technology and active learning methods, whereas past education relied heavily on rote memorization and textbooks.
