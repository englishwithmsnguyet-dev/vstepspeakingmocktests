Topic: There are a lot of disadvantages to keeping animals in zoos.
  • lack of space
  • lack of survival skills
  • loss of freedom
(Chủ đề: Có rất nhiều bất lợi khi nuôi nhốt động vật trong sở thú.)
  • thiếu không gian
  • thiếu kỹ năng sinh tồn
  • mất tự do
SAMPLE ANSWERS (gợi ý bài mẫu)
B1 LEVEL
There are some drawbacks of keeping animals in zoos. One major disadvantage is lack of space. This is because animals are kept in cages and can't move around freely like in the wild. As a result, they may feel uncomfortable and stressed. Another disadvantage is lack of survival skills. In fact, since animals get food and medical care from humans every day, they don't need to hunt. Therefore, they may find it hard to survive if they return to the wild. One more disadvantage is loss of freedom. This means animals are taken away from their natural homes and habits. This can harm their physical and mental health over time. In short, keeping animals in zoos has several clear drawbacks, as mentioned above.
