SITUATION
Your friend feels very stressed with her studies and wants to do something to relax. Which one would you recommend her: taking a sport, learning new soft skills, or doing volunteer work?
B1 LEVEL
Well, if I had to suggest something for my friend to help her relax, I would recommend taking a sport.
First of all, it’s an entertaining activity because she can have fun and enjoy her free time. It’s also a good way to reduce stress. So, she can feel more relaxed.
Secondly, it’s beneficial for health because it allows her to stay active. Besides, it helps her improve both physical and mental health. So, she can maintain a healthy lifestyle.
I wouldn’t recommend learning new soft skills because it may require a lot of time and effort. As for doing volunteer work, it’s less suitable because it may be tiring after a long day of studying.
